## Build your setup

Start by understanding where configuration lives and how the installer resolves module implementations. These guides take you from a blueprint fork to a small, reviewed setup you can verify on one machine.

## Customize the pieces

Bring your own dotfiles, add a focused module, and separate the responsibilities of your machines. Keep preferences and platform differences explicit so you can review them before the next installation.
